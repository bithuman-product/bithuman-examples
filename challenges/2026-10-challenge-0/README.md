# Builder Challenge #0: give a showcase character a job

**Entries: 00:00 UTC Tuesday, October 6, 2026 to 23:59 UTC Sunday, October 11, 2026.**

Pick a character from the bitHuman public showcase and give it a job: a museum guide, an onboarding buddy, a help-desk greeter, a kiosk host. Your call.

**Enter any one of three ways:**

1. **Discord:** a new post in the #challenge forum of the [bitHuman Discord](https://discord.gg/x3tMhJvX4X).
2. **X or Bluesky:** a public post with **#bitHumanChallenge**, a link to what you built and a clip of up to 60 seconds.
3. **GitHub:** an issue in this repository, using the [Challenge entry form](https://github.com/bithuman-product/bithuman-examples/issues/new?template=challenge-entry.yml).

**Joining the bitHuman Discord is not required to enter or win.**

**Prizes:** on Friday, October 16, 2026 the bitHuman Team picks 3 winners on the judging criteria below. Each gets 2,000 bitHuman credits, added to their bitHuman account balance: enough to create your own Expression 2 avatar. Credits are not cash. Votes, likes and stars don't count.

Entrants must be 18 or older. One entry per person. No real person's face or voice without their written consent. No purchase necessary.

New to bitHuman? Sample avatars you can try without an account: [docs.bithuman.ai/start](https://docs.bithuman.ai/start). Essence 2 renders a photoreal person from one portrait; Expression 2 renders any character (people, animals, cartoons) from one portrait. The examples in this repository are a good place to start building.

- **Your own work.** You made it, or your team did.
- **Your rights.** You own or have permission for everything in it: the characters, images, voice, music and script. A voice must be your own, licensed, or from a voice service whose terms allow this use (bitHuman provides no voices).

- **No third-party characters** (from films, games, shows or brands) unless you hold a license that covers this use.
- **Within the rules.** Nothing that breaks the law, the rules of the platform you post on, the bitHuman Discord server rules (for Discord entries) or the [bitHuman Terms](https://www.bithuman.ai/legal/terms). Nothing directed at children, and no real minors.

## 7. Judging

The bitHuman Team picks the winners on these criteria: an AI model scores every eligible entry and drafts the picks, and the team can review and change that draft for at least 24 hours before the winners are listed.

| Criterion | Weight | What we look for |
|---|---|---|
| The job | 30% | The character does a clear, useful job for a real person |
| Craft | 30% | How well it works: the conversation, the timing, how the character fits the job |
| Originality | 25% | A fresh idea, or a fresh take on a familiar one |
| Show it | 15% | The clip and the link let us see it working |

**Votes, likes, reactions, follows, reposts, stars and shares never count**, on any platform. Ties go to the higher Craft score, then to the entry posted first. The bitHuman Team's decisions are final, except where the law says otherwise. If there are fewer than three eligible entries, we may award fewer prizes.
