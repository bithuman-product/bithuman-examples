setup.sh fills this folder with three things:

  agent.imx          the avatar: GET /v1/agent/{code}/model/download?model=expression-2
  shared-engine.imx  the shared Expression 2 engine file, one for every avatar
                     (release expression2-engine-mac-arm64-1.0.0; the mac file is
                     the right one for iPhone too)
  speech16k.wav      16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM speech for the "Speak" button.

The whole folder is copied into the app bundle as a folder reference, so the
app reads it at Bundle.main.url(forResource: "Model", withExtension: nil).
